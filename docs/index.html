FLEET STRIKE

Hide your fleet. Hunt theirs. A two-player naval battle for Android phones and tablets, or right in your browser.

Download for Android Play in your browser
Android app: latest version · free · Android 8.0 or newer (Amazon Fire tablets too) · about 8 MB
Browser game: any phone, tablet or computer, no install. Play a friend nearby on their own device, pass one device back and forth, or practise against the computer.

On a computer? Scan this with your phone or tablet's camera to open this page there.

Phones too

On a phone the board you fire at sits on top and your own fleet below it. Turn the phone sideways to see both side by side. A phone and a tablet can play each other.

The game turns with the device even when rotation lock is on. To keep it one way up, choose Landscape or Portrait under Screen on the home screen.

Install on a phone or tablet

  1. Open this page on the device and tap Download for Android.
  2. Open the downloaded FleetStrike.apk (from the notification or the Downloads app).
  3. If Android asks, let your browser install unknown apps, then go back and tap Install.
  4. If Play Protect warns about an unknown app, tap More details → Install anyway.

Install it on both devices. To update later, just install the new download over the old one.

Play a friend

  • Wi-Fi: both devices on the same Wi-Fi (or one sharing a hotspot). One taps Host game, the other taps Join game and picks it from the list. If it doesn't appear, type the address shown on the host.
  • Bluetooth: no Wi-Fi needed. One taps Host game and allows it to be visible; the other taps Join game, allows Nearby devices, and picks the host.
  • Practice: play the computer on Easy, Normal or Hard.

How to play

  • Each player secretly places five ships: Carrier (5), Battleship (4), Cruiser (3), Submarine (3), Destroyer (2).
  • Take turns calling a square on the other player's grid. Red fire is a hit, white rings a miss.
  • Sink all five enemy ships to win. Optional house rule for the host: a hit earns another shot.

No peeking

Each tablet keeps its own fleet. When you lock in your layout, only a sealed fingerprint of it is sent. At the end both fleets are revealed and checked against every reported hit and miss, so a cheater gets caught.
